Where can you actually smoke in Las Vegas?

● LIVE9
Quick answer

You can buy legally, but public use is illegal and most Strip hotels ban cannabis, even in smoking rooms. Your legal options: a licensed consumption lounge or a private residence that allows it. Lounge rule: you buy on site, you use on site, nothing leaves.

Coming

Licensed retail sales are scheduled to begin July 1, 2027. Hampton Roads listings go live the day stores open.
